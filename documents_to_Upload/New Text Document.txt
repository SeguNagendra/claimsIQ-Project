4. Adjuster review checklist
For this scenario, the adjuster can assess the following:
- Itemized hospital bill and proof of payment.
- Discharge summary and hospitalization details.
- Doctor's prescriptions and treatment records.
- Diagnostic reports supporting the fever and viral infection diagnosis.
- Policy validity, coverage limits, and waiting periods.
- Applicable exclusions and any required pre-authorization.
These are review points for the scenario, not a confirmation that the claim is approved or that every item is required under the actual policy.

6. Suggested client-demo narration
We created a hospitalization claim for ₹25,000 for a customer who received treatment for fever and a viral infection. After creating the claim, we assigned it to an adjuster, moved it to Under Review with a justification, and added an adjuster note.
We then used ClaimIQ's AI-powered policy search to identify the documents the adjuster should verify and the policy conditions or exclusions that need to be reviewed before making a decision. This demonstrates how ClaimIQ combines claim workflow management with AI-assisted policy information retrieval.
